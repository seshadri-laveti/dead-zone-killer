# Safety, Radio Compliance, and Responsible Testing

- Verify the current local rules for the selected radio band, output power, bandwidth, duty cycle, and antenna.
- Do not assume 433 MHz or any other band is automatically legal for every device/use case.
- Do not deploy an aerial balloon without reviewing aviation restrictions, weather, tethering, payload mass, recovery, and landowner permissions.
- Keep tests at ground level until a documented safety review is complete.
- Protect battery packs against short circuits, overcharge, overheating, and mechanical damage.
- This prototype is not a certified life-safety device. Emergency responders must not rely on it without formal validation and authorization.
